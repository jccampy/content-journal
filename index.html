Spacing rule

The hard floor. The same place will never be scheduled into two posts closer together than this — and it counts your real posting history too, not just what's on the calendar. If you move something by hand into a gap that's too tight, the calendar flags it rather than blocking you.

Rollout across platforms

✨ Claude AI ideas optional

The calendar works fully without this — the built-in Atlanta engine generates every idea on its own. Adding an Anthropic API key lets the ✨ AI ideas button write fresh themes, hooks and captions on top of what you already have.

About your key: it is saved only in this browser, never in the GitHub repo, and never sent anywhere except directly to Anthropic. Don't paste it into a shared or public computer. You can revoke it any time at console.anthropic.com.
